01

What Orbit processes

Orbit analyzes text that you paste in order to suggest a task title, category, deadline, time, amount, and reference. This analysis happens in your browser. The beta does not send pasted text to an Orbit server.

When you confirm a task, Orbit stores the task fields—not the original pasted notice—in your browser’s local storage. Settings such as region, date format, and onboarding completion are also stored locally.

The beta does not include
  • User accounts or a cloud database
  • Advertising or third-party analytics
  • Document uploads or background transmission
  • Automatic legal or immigration decisions
02

Storage, retention, and your control

Tasks remain in the browser until you delete them, clear site data, or use Orbit’s “Delete all local data” control. Removing the app or clearing browser storage may permanently delete tasks.

You can export a JSON backup and import it later. Backups are ordinary, unencrypted files that may contain task details. Store them somewhere you trust.

If you use an email link to contact support, your email provider—not Orbit’s local app—will process that message under its own terms.

03

Safety and security

Do not paste Social Security numbers, full passport numbers, banking credentials, card numbers, passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary medical information. Redact notices before using Smart Inbox.

Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to view locally stored tasks. Orbit cannot recover information after local storage is cleared.

Free private-beta terms

Orbit organizes information. It does not provide immigration, legal, financial, tax, medical, or government advice. Suggestions may be incomplete or wrong. You are responsible for checking every result against the original notice and authoritative sources.
